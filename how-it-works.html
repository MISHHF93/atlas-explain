How ATLAS Explain works

ATLAS Explain is an educational companion to the ATLAS research desk. It explains trading terms and market moves in plain language and never tells anyone to trade.

Select text, get a plain explanation

  1. Highlight a term or a sentence on any page: a news story, a chart, a filing.
  2. Click the ATLAS Explain icon, press Alt+Shift+X, or right-click and choose Explain.
  3. Read a plain answer with two sides, then ask for it simpler, with an example, or why it matters. Open the side panel to keep it beside the page.

Hybrid AI: on your device first

  1. Your highlightSelected text, page title and site name. Nothing else.
  2. Gemini Nano, on this deviceTried first in desktop Chrome. Private notes never leave the browser.
  3. Gemini in the cloud, if neededFirebase AI Logic with App Check. Private notes are dropped.

Every answer says where it ran. Gemini Nano runs inside desktop Chrome (version 139 or newer, on a computer that meets Chrome's on-device model requirements). Phones, tablets and other browsers do not have Gemini Nano, so they use Gemini in the cloud through Firebase AI Logic, and the app labels those answers "Cloud". App Check protects the cloud route, and each browser has a fair-use limit.

Where the numbers come from

  • Live crypto prices come from a public market-data provider. Its name is shown next to the prices and links to its site.
  • Today's digest and ATLAS leans come from ATLAS running on your own computer. The hosted app cannot read your computer, so on the web these show "Connect ATLAS" or "not ready" instead.
  • Lights stay grey. A light only turns green or red when a paper model clears a published evidence gate. Today every lean is withheld, because the models were trained on feeds that do not allow it. The app says so.
  • No made-up numbers. Missing data stays missing, with the reason.

Add to Chrome

Chrome Web Store listing: coming soon. When it is live, this button will take you straight to it. Until then, install the same build from the download:

Download the extension (.zip)

  1. Unzip the file into a folder you will keep, for example Documents/atlas-explain.
  2. Open chrome://extensions and turn on Developer mode (top right).
  3. Choose Load unpacked and select the unzipped folder.
  4. Pin ATLAS Explain from the puzzle icon. Highlight a term on any page and click the icon, or press Alt+Shift+X.

Prefer to build it yourself? The source is at github.com/MISHHF93/atlas-explain (npm ci, then npm run build:extension). Sign in on this site in the same Chrome profile and the extension uses the same account.

On a phone or a browser without extensions, use the web app. It is the same Explain, Assets and Learn, with answers from the cloud model, clearly labelled.

What it never does

  • It never reads balances, account numbers, positions or form fields.
  • It never sends private notes or watchlists to the cloud, and never stores them.
  • It never gives trade instructions.
