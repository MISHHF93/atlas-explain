Welcome to ATLAS Explain

Two quick steps, about 30 seconds. Then highlight anything you read and get it in plain language.

1Pin it to your toolbar

Click the puzzle-piece icon at the top right of Chrome, then the pin next to ATLAS Explain. Its icon now stays in your toolbar, one click away.

2Try it right here

Highlight any phrase in the passage below, for example stop-limit order. Then press Alt+Shift+X, or right-click and choose Explain with ATLAS Explain.

The stock gapped down at the open on heavy volume, then filled half of the gap by the close.

A stop-limit order becomes a limit order once the stop price trades, so in a fast market it can stay unfilled.

Implied volatility rose into the earnings date: options were pricing in a bigger move, without saying which direction.

The 10-year Treasury yield rose 5 basis points, and the yield curve stayed inverted.

The answer appears in the ATLAS Explain popup. When Chrome supports it, it's written right on your device and nothing is sent.

3Optional: sign in

Signing in keeps your explanations and watchlist on every device. You can skip this and use ATLAS Explain without an account.
