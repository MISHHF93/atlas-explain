ATLAS Explain lives in your Chrome toolbar. Highlight jargon on any page, a news story, a chart, a filing, and get a calm, two-sided explanation. It runs on your device first, and it never reads your balances or accounts.

How it works

  1. 1

    Select text on any page

    Highlight a term or a sentence, then click the toolbar icon, press Alt+Shift+X, or right-click and choose Explain.

  2. 2

    Get a plain explanation

    What it means, what some read as opportunity, what others read as risk, and what would change the picture. Never a trade call.

  3. 3

    On your device first

    Gemini Nano answers inside Chrome when your computer has it. Otherwise Gemini answers in the cloud, and the app tells you which one ran.

The popup and the side panel

Private by design

  • Reads only what you select. The highlighted text, the page title and the site name, and only when you ask.
  • Never balances or accounts. It does not read form fields, account numbers, positions or balances, on any site.
  • No made-up numbers. Missing data stays missing, with the reason. Every price names its source.
  • Education only. Two sides, every time. It never tells anyone to trade.

Read the privacy policy

Questions

Is it free?

Yes. ATLAS Explain is free during the beta. Paid plans are built but switched off, and nothing is charged.

Does it give trading advice?

No. It is education only. Every answer shows two sides and what would change the picture. It never tells anyone to trade.

What does it read on a page?

Only the text you highlight, the page title and the site name, and only when you ask for an explanation. It never reads balances, account numbers, positions or form fields.

Where does the AI run?

On your device first, with Gemini Nano in desktop Chrome when your computer supports it. Otherwise Gemini answers in the cloud through Firebase AI Logic with App Check. Every answer says which one ran.
