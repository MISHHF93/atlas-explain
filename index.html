How it works

  1. 1

    Select text on any page

    Highlight a term or a sentence, then click the toolbar icon, press Alt+Shift+X, or right-click and choose Explain.

  2. 2

    Get a plain explanation

    What it means, what some read as opportunity, what others read as risk, and what would change the picture. Never a trade call.

  3. 3

    On your device first

    Gemini Nano answers inside Chrome when your computer supports it. Otherwise Gemini answers in the cloud, and every answer tells you which one ran.

Hybrid AI you can see

Only the text you select is used, and it's answered on your device whenever your browser can. Private notes never leave the browser.

Gemini Nano works in recent desktop Chrome on computers that meet Chrome's requirements. Phones, tablets and other browsers use Gemini in the cloud, and those answers are labelled "Cloud". Cloud answers have a fair daily limit; answers on your device do not.

The popup and the side panel

Private by design

  • Reads only what you select. The highlighted text, the page title and the site name, and only when you ask.
  • Never balances or accounts. It does not read form fields, account numbers, positions or balances, on any site.
  • No made-up numbers. Missing data stays missing, with the reason. Every price names its source and links to it.
  • No predictions. It shows prices and explains them. It never says where a price is going.
  • Education only. Two sides, every time. It never tells anyone to trade.

Open the web app

Add to Chrome from the Chrome Web Store

  1. Choose Add to Chrome on the store page.
  2. Pin ATLAS Explain from the puzzle icon. Highlight a term on any page and click the icon, or press Alt+Shift+X.

Signing in is optional. If you sign in on this site in the same Chrome profile, the extension uses the same account.

The extension needs desktop Chrome. On a phone, use the web app; its answers come from the cloud model and say so.

Also in your AI assistant

Add ATLAS Explain as a connector in Claude or another assistant that supports MCP. It can then use plain-language terms, asset details and official figures, live crypto prices and past moves, and today's headlines while you chat, each with its source. Free, read-only, no account.
