Order ticket help

Stop-limit order. A stop-limit order becomes a limit order once the stop price trades. In a fast market it can stay unfilled if the price gaps through the limit.

Bid-ask spread. The spread widened after the close, so a market order may fill away from the last quote.

Time in force. Good-til-cancelled (GTC) orders stay working across sessions until they fill or are cancelled.
