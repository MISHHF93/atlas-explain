Privacy policy

Last updated 14 November 2026

ATLAS Explain explains finance terms and market news in plain language. It is built to use as little of your data as possible: only the text you choose to explain, and only when you ask.

Who we are

ATLAS Explain (the website at atlas-explain.web.app, its web app and its Chrome extension) is run by the ATLAS Explain project, based in Ontario, Canada. To reach us about privacy, use the Support page. You can download or delete your data yourself at any time from Account.

What we never collect

  • No balances, account numbers, positions or form fields, on any website, ever.
  • No page content beyond the text you highlight, plus the page title and the site's name, and only when you ask for an explanation.
  • No advertising. Your information is never sold, and never shared for advertising.

What we collect, and why

  • Text you ask about. When you ask for an explanation, the text you highlighted or typed, the page title and the site name are used to write the answer. When your browser supports it, this happens on your own device with Gemini Nano and nothing is sent. Otherwise the text is sent to Google's Gemini model in the cloud to write the answer. Account-like numbers are removed before anything is sent.
  • History. Your explanation history, watchlist and recently viewed assets are stored in your browser. If you sign in, your watchlist, settings and saved explanations are stored in your account so they follow you to other devices.
  • Account. If you sign in with Google or an email link: an account id, your email address and display name, and how you signed in.
  • A daily usage count of cloud explanations (a date and a number) for each browser or account, to keep the free service fair for everyone.
  • Security checks. To stop abuse of cloud answers, Google reCAPTCHA (through Firebase App Check) checks that requests come from a real browser on our site. Google processes signals such as your IP address for this.
  • Usage statistics, only if you allow them (see Cookies and analytics).

Where your data is processed

We use Google Firebase (hosting, sign-in, database, Remote Config, App Check and AI Logic with Gemini) and, if you allow it, Google Analytics. Google processes this data for us as a service provider, and it may be processed outside Canada, including in the United States.

To show prices, images and official figures, your browser also loads content directly from: CoinGecko (crypto prices and coin images), Logo.dev (company logos, when shown), and our own data site, atlas-explain-data.web.app (official rates and figures). These providers see your IP address when your browser loads from them, as with any website. We send them no other information about you.

Cookies, storage and analytics

ATLAS Explain stores a few items in your browser to work: your theme, history, watchlist, recently viewed assets, and whether you've seen the welcome steps. Firebase stores sign-in information so you stay signed in.

Anonymous usage statistics are off unless you allow them. If we turn them on, the web app asks you once. If you say yes, Google Analytics sets cookies and receives a short list of events, such as "an explanation was ready, on the device" or "the watchlist was used", plus error codes. It never receives what you highlight, type or save, prices or asset names, and advertising features are turned off. You can change your answer any time in Account under "Usage statistics". The Chrome extension sends no usage statistics.

Your choices and rights

  • Download your data and delete your account yourself in Account. Deleting your account removes your saved explanations, settings, watchlist and past usage counts, then the account itself.
  • Use ATLAS Explain without an account. You can also clear this browser's data from Account.
  • Depending on where you live (for example under Canada's PIPEDA, the EU and UK GDPR, or California law), you may have further rights, such as to access, correct or object. Contact us through the Support page, and we'll answer within 30 days.

How long we keep data

Account data stays until you delete your account. History kept in your browser stays until you clear it. When you delete your account, today's usage count (a date and a number) can't be removed straight away, because it's what keeps the daily free allowance fair. It can no longer be linked to you.

Children

ATLAS Explain is not meant for children under 16, and we don't knowingly collect their data.

Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update the date above. For significant changes we'll also tell you in the app.

ATLAS Explain gives general education about finance. It is not investment, financial, tax or legal advice. See the Terms.
