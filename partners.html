ATLAS Explain for businesses

Plain-language market explanations and sourced official figures for your app and your AI agents: the same content ATLAS Explain gives in Claude, by REST API or MCP. Education only. Every fact names its source and time, and nothing recommends, rates or forecasts anything.

Status: free sandbox, open now. Pilots and paid plans come after a legal review of the partner terms.

Who it's for

Banks and credit unions

Investment-scam warning checks, plain-language explainers and a calendar of rate decisions and data releases, inside your own app.

Brokers and advisers

Evidence briefs with every fact sourced, and client education that never reads as advice.

Fintech apps and AI agents

A REST API for your app, and an MCP connector your AI assistant can call directly.

Financial-wellness and education platforms

Money maths, a learning path and live official figures to make lessons real.

Every answer is { data, meta }. meta.credit says what to show with the data, and meta.educationOnly is always true.
