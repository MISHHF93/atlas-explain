Frequently asked questions

How do I add it to my AI assistant?

Add one address as a connector in Claude, ChatGPT, Cursor, VS Code or any assistant that supports MCP. The address and the steps for each assistant are on the setup page. No account or key is needed.

Is it free?

Yes. The connector, explaining, learning and live prices are free. A Pro plan with more cloud answers in the web app may be offered later, and you will always see the price before paying anything.

Does it give trading advice?

No. It is education only. It explains terms and shows sources, and it never tells anyone to trade or says where a price is going.

What does the connector receive?

Only what your assistant sends when it uses an ATLAS Explain tool, such as a term or a ticker. It never sees your chat history, accounts or files, changes nothing and keeps none of it.

Where do the figures and prices come from?

Official figures come from government and regulator sources, crypto prices from a public market-data provider, and headlines from public news feeds. Each answer names its source and time. Missing data stays missing, with the reason.

Can I use it without an AI assistant?

Yes. The web app at atlas-explain.web.app/app explains whatever you type or paste. In desktop Chrome it tries Gemini Nano on your device first; otherwise Gemini answers in the cloud. Every answer says which one ran.

Do I need an account?

For the web app, yes: a free account, with Google or a one-time email link. It keeps your saved explanations and settings on every device. The AI-assistant connector needs no account.

If something looks stuck

  • Your assistant can't reach ATLAS Explain: check the address on the setup page, then remove the connector and add it again.
  • "Cloud" answers do not arrive in the web app: check your connection. Each browser has a fair-use limit of 8 cloud answers a minute and 300 a day; on-device answers are never limited.
  • Answers always say "Cloud": Gemini Nano needs recent desktop Chrome on a supported computer. When it isn't available, the cloud answers instead and says so.
  • The site looks out of date: reload with Ctrl+Shift+R (Cmd+Shift+R on a Mac).
