Frequently asked questions

Is it free?

Yes. ATLAS Explain is free during the beta. Paid plans are built but switched off, and nothing is charged.

Does it give trading advice?

No. It is education only. Every answer shows two sides and what would change the picture. It never tells anyone to trade.

What does it read on a page?

Only the text you highlight, the page title and the site name, and only when you ask for an explanation. It never reads balances, account numbers, positions or form fields.

Where does the AI run?

On your device first, with Gemini Nano in desktop Chrome when your computer supports it. Otherwise Gemini answers in the cloud through Firebase AI Logic with App Check. Every answer says which one ran.

Why are the lights grey?

A light only turns green or red when a paper research model clears a published evidence gate. Today every lean is withheld, because the models were trained on data feeds that do not allow it. The app says so instead of guessing.

Where do the prices come from?

From a public market-data provider, named and linked next to the prices. Missing prices stay missing.

Do I need an account?

No. It works signed out. Signing in with Google or an email link keeps your saved explanations across devices.

Does it work on my phone?

The extension needs desktop Chrome. On a phone, open the web app at atlas-explain.web.app/app; answers there come from the cloud model and are labelled.

When will it be on the Chrome Web Store?

Soon. Until the listing is live you can install it from the download on the How it works page.

If something looks stuck

  • "Cloud" answers do not arrive: check your connection. Each browser has a fair-use limit of 8 cloud answers a minute and 300 a day; on-device answers are never limited.
  • On-device is not offered: Gemini Nano needs desktop Chrome 139 or newer on a supported computer. The cloud answers instead and says so.
  • The site looks out of date: reload with Ctrl+Shift+R (Cmd+Shift+R on a Mac).

Educational information, not investment advice. ATLAS Explain never tells anyone to trade.
